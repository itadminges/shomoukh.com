ABOUT
Welcome to Shomoukh Nursery Schools
Shomoukh Early Childhood Education is an International Early Years Nursery School, inspired by the Reggio Emilia approach,
originated in Italy. In our school, children are viewed as unique, capable, resilient, creative, curious, and intelligent. We
embrace everyone&#39;s culture while offering children a wide range of opportunities to become confident and prepare them to meet
future challenges.
Our students are active citizens who reach their full potential within a safe, healthy, nurturing, and aesthetic environment that
respects their rights and opinions. Teaching methods are based on children&#39;s needs and interests, ensuring that every child is
actively involved in the learning process and receives personal attention and guidance.
Our School&#39;s Philosophy
Our school&#39;s ethos and culture promote positive relations among its members and provides opportunities so that each child
feels fulfilled. We strive to create a happy environment that emphasizes the social, emotional, physical, and mental
development of each child.
We believe that partnerships between the school, parents, and the community play an important role in the child&#39;s educational
journey. Families are invited to participate in school-related activities, initiate projects, and lend their support in events.
Our Mission
Our mission is to provide high-quality childcare services and promote the development of creativity and self-confidence of
children in a positive, friendly, and respectful learning environment to prepare them for a lifelong learning experience. We value
partnerships between the nursery, parents, and the community, and ensure that all children are actively involved in the learning
process.
Our Vision
Our Vision at Shomoukh nursery school is to create a happy and fun environment that emphasizes the social, emotional,
physical, and mental development of each child.
Our Values
We value the excellence in caring and development of every child as an individual. We strive to ensure high spirits of optimism,
collegiality, and the involvement of the wider community. We provide continuous and ongoing professional development as a
right and duty of every individual and group involved in the education of children.
Core Principles
Community: At Shomoukh, we believe in diversity while deeply respecting Omani values. We organize various activities and
events throughout the academic year to build a strong sense of community.
Giving Back: Our school has close ties with the local community and organizes various events during the academic year to
support those in need.
Environment: We constantly promote environmental sustainability and awareness through our activities, teaching the
importance of Reducing, Reusing, and Recycling.

Health &amp; Safety: We make your child&#39;s health and safety our top priority. Our standards are the highest in the industry, meeting
or exceeding all local and state guidelines.

PROGRAMS
Our Promise to Families
At Shomoukh, we know childhood is a treasured time. We believe it is our responsibility to provide environments and
experiences that capture the joys of childhood, nurture each child&#39;s individual growth and development, and pave the way for
success in school and life.
The Shomoukh Difference
Shomoukh operates twelve (12) months per year, following a three-term academic year and a fun-filled summer camp, with
various learning opportunities embedded within the day. Working in small groups, our students explore their environment,
improve their skills, and enhance their knowledge in various areas.
We offer a unique early education experience built on a proven history of expertise, and on what working families have told us
they want and need for their children and for themselves.
Three Pillars
Reggio Emilia
Thoughtfully and carefully designed nursery schools, programs, and schedules – all created with the needs of both children and
parents in mind.
Inspired Teachers
Dedicated early childhood professionals fully engaged in teaching careers, who benefit from our award-winning training and
ongoing support.
Proven Approach to Education
Research-informed curriculum designed by experienced educators to inspire children not just to learn, but to think on their own.
Our Programs
Shomoukh follows the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) curriculum, a UK-based system of education that sets the goals in
terms of learning and development for children from birth to five years of age. It also sets out the standards for ensuring the
welfare of all students within safe and supportive environments.
The EYFS program integrates well with the Reggio Emilia philosophy, which sets the standards for project-based education,
where each child is driven by their own interests. It is a student-centered approach where parents, teachers, and children are
equal partners in the learning journey.
The Teacher
Teachers at Shomoukh Nursery Schools are researchers, learning alongside the children. They observe and document the
children&#39;s learning, whilst nurturing their curiosity and encouraging their thoughts, ideas, and actions through open-ended
activities and explorations.
The Environment: &quot;The Third Teacher&quot;
The classrooms and outdoor areas are spaces that offer provocations for learning, exploration, and play. Spaces are
interconnected to support communication, curiosity, and interaction between children and adults. The environment is rich in
opportunities and is modified based on the projects and learning experiences. Care for spaces and resources is viewed as an
educational component and safety is a priority.
Additional Focus Areas:
• Second Language
• Movement Matters
• Handwriting

ENRICHMENTS
Shomoukh Fun Space: Smart &amp; Innovative Learning
Shomoukh Ateliers
Shomoukh ateliers are viewed as spaces where a group of children work together, using their imagination, creativity, and
knowledge to experiment with different materials, technologies, and tools, under the encouragement and supervision of the
Atelierista (artist). The Atelier is not just an art corner but rather a place of research where kids are invited to experiment.
Clay Atelier
Children are experimenting with clay, a soft and natural material with a smooth or floury texture that can be shaped by the
gestures of their hand or using various tools.
Art Atelier
Children have access to stimulating resources and they are encouraged to use multiple ways and symbolic languages, tools,
and materials to express themselves.
Digital Atelier
Children use light in different forms as well as analogical and digital tools to investigate and research new connections, learning
strategies, and explore the area of logic and imagination.
Our Activities
Project
At Shomoukh, we follow a project-based approach that helps children further explore their ideas and make connections with the
world around them. These ideas are normally generated through brainstorming and careful observations of each child&#39;s
individual interests.
Tiny Town
A pretend play area that helps children learn about themselves and balance their own ideas with others. Pretend play requires
thinking strategies and promotes the development of skills and knowledge.
Sensory
Children are engaged in activities that promote play, creativity, and exploration, while stimulating their senses. Sensory play
plays an important role in brain development as it encourages the use of problem-solving strategies and supports language
development.
Music
Music activities balance the body and mind and also improve children&#39;s ability to learn. Our children are exposed to music from
a very young age to develop all of their potentials and talents.
Drama
Through role playing, our students act out assigned roles related to favorite stories. Puppetry is also a beloved form of art.
These activities promote communication, improve social skills, and help students become more confident in life from an early
age.
ECA &amp; PE
Through Extra Curricular Activities including Physical Education and Sports, our children are given opportunities to explore new
interests, make new friends, and build various skills in different environments.
